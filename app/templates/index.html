    >_ [ ARCHITECTURE & LAB NOTES ]

    [ 01 // LINE-BREAK DE-HYPHENATION ]

    Typographic PDFs break words across line endings with hyphens. The engine automatically recombines fractured stems, preserving lexical continuity required for RAG embeddings and semantic search.

    [ 02 // C-ENGINE STREAM OPTIMIZATION ]

    PyMuPDF operates at the PDF object-tree level in C, eliminating duplicate font resources, deflating streams, and optimizing embedded imagery without compromising vector text sharpness.

    [ 03 // COMPLETE ISOLATION & PRIVACY ]

    Zero external calls. No tokens or byte-streams are transmitted to third-party cloud services. All operations execute strictly in local RAM for the time required to perform the task.
